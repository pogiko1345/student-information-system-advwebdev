# NU MOA branding — Yap

The unmodified National University logo asset was obtained from the official [NU MOA campus page](https://www.nu.edu.ph/campus-page/nu-moa). The separate “MOA Campus” text identifies the campus without altering the mark. The asset is stored locally at `frontend/public/nu-logo.webp` so it displays even when the university site is unavailable.
